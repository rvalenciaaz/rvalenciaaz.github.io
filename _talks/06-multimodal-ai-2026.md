I presented this work at the Fourth Workshop on Multimodal AI, held at UCL East Campus on 11–12 June 2026, and received the **Best Pitch** award.

The pitch concerns adapting multi-modal variational autoencoders for microbiome data. The title is listed in the organisers’ [accepted abstracts](https://multimodalai.github.io/multimodalai26/accepted-abstracts/), and the [UKOMAIN award announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ukomain-news-august-2026-ukomain-c0bye) includes a link to the recording.
