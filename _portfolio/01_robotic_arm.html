Watch the robot in action.

Opentrons is a flexible platform, but operations such as moving tubes and opening ChiBio reactor lids need an extra arm. I built this one to reach across the deck while remaining small enough to fit inside the Opentrons OT-2.

The design is by Kelton Serra, whose YouTube channel is Build Some Stuff. He kindly shares the 3D models, an overview video, and two assembly tutorials: part one and part two.

The gripper uses a microservo, the shoulder joint uses a 20 kg servo to support the arm's weight, and the wrist, elbow and base use RC servos. Everything connects to an Arduino-compatible servo controller powered by an adjustable power adapter. An Arduino Uno links the controller to the input potentiometers, with a small script aligning their angle readings to the servo shaft angles.
