--- title: "Building the open-source Inkwell" excerpt: "Assembling a simple mechanical device for producing consistent biological smears." collection: portfolio image: /assets/images/inkwell.webp topic: "Open hardware" ---

Building Inkwell was an amazing experience. It is a non-electrical device for producing consistent biological smears, usually of blood, though it also works with microbial cultures.

The core mechanism is a spring connected to a syringe plunger. A valve controls the air flowing out of the syringe. At the right setting, restricted airflow dampens the spring action, making the motion approximately constant in speed.

The design comes from the Manu Prakash lab at Stanford. Their project announcement and preprint cover the assembly, mechanical details and field testing.
