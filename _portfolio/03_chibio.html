A new year means general maintenance of the ChiBio system. I flushed 70% ethanol through four miniature bioreactors to clean the system before the next experiments.

The pumps work in opposing directions to keep reservoir levels approximately constant throughout cleaning. I also tested new cross-shaped stirring bars.
