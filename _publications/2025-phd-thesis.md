My PhD thesis, held in the University of Edinburgh’s [Edinburgh Research Archive](https://era.ed.ac.uk/handle/1842/43540), under the full author name Ricardo Gabriel Valencia Albornoz.

**Title:** *Metabolomics and machine learning to assist biotechnology culture optimisation*.

The thesis combines active learning and Bayesian optimisation with metabolomics to refine culture-medium conditions for surfactin production in *Bacillus*. It also explores automated preparation of seven-component media and multi-objective optimisation.
