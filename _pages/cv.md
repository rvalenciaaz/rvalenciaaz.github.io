## Computational Genomics Scientist · EIT Oxford

I work in the Pathogen Program at the Ellison Institute of Technology Oxford, based in Oxford, UK. [Current LinkedIn profile]({{ site.author.linkedin }}).

## Previous postdoctoral research · Earlham Institute

## PhD · University of Edinburgh

Research at IQB3, School of Biological Sciences, combining active learning for iterative experimentation, metabolomics, robotics and automation towards a prototype automated biochemist. Supervised by Karl Burgess and Diego Oyarzún.

My thesis, [*Metabolomics and machine learning to assist biotechnology culture optimisation*](https://era.ed.ac.uk/handle/1842/43540), is available through Edinburgh Research Archive (repository record dated June 2025).
